# Morrow & Mason — market assumptions and product price memo

## Product decision

Morrow & Mason is a product preview for an independent residential remodeling studio. Its public
surface is quote-first and pre-launch: it demonstrates how a homeowner can review work, compare
materials, shape a project scope, and prepare a handoff. It does not claim that a contractor,
designer, estimator, payment processor, permit reviewer, or scheduling system is connected.

## Recommended CGC product price

Internal implementation recommendation: **US$12,900** for the product website scope represented by
this preview. This is a price for a website product, not an investment, ROI promise, family price, or
the price of a remodeling job. It must remain in this internal memo and not appear in the Morrow & Mason
funnel until separately approved as a product price.

The planning assumption is approximately 104 hours at about US$124/hour. It covers the multi-view
experience, bilingual content system, local visual assets, interactive material explorer, scope builder,
stateful validation, responsive/accessibility work, policy gates, and official QA handoff. It excludes
real scheduling, payments, permit verification, CRM/estimating integrations, photography production,
domain, hosting fees, ongoing content operations, and legal review.

## Why this is deeper than a landing page

- Home explains the studio promise and makes the quote-first boundary clear.
- Work provides navigable case studies and a visual filter.
- Material Lab lets a visitor compare finish families and see the board update.
- Scope Builder records a local project brief, exposes validation and an editable summary, and ends in
  a clearly marked no-send handoff.
- Policies, privacy, terms, cookies, and scope pages expose the pre-launch/legal gate instead of
  presenting placeholder language as an active policy.

## Market references

Current vendor references show that remodeling/design workflows commonly include selections, moodboards,
3D or visual planning, estimates/proposals, schedules, budgets, change orders, online payments, and
client dashboards. Houzz Pro's current published plans describe Design at $99/month, Pro at $199/month,
and Teams from $399/month, with features such as selections, estimates, proposals, schedules, budgets,
and client dashboards; the exact plan/pricing can change and is not a quote for this website.

Squarespace's current pricing page positions Core as a service-business plan and lists forms, SEO/mobile
optimization, customer accounts, automated taxes/shipping, and other commerce/lead tools. Those platform
features support the decision to model a real conversion journey, but they do not replace the product
specific discovery and visual selection work in this preview.

## Legal and operational gate

The policy pages are intentionally marked pre-launch and not effective. Before a real publication,
the product owner must supply legal name, business address/jurisdiction, contact channel, data vendors,
retention/deletion rules, booking/cancellation terms, payment/refund terms, rights to photos and project
materials, and any licensing/permit claims. No missing facts are invented here.
